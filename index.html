Help

This application is an analysis tool only. It connects to public Deriv market data, downloads historical ticks and monitors live ticks.

For a 100-tick duration, the analyzer checks the digit approximately 100 ticks after each historical entry point. This is different from simply counting the most recent digits.

NO SIGNAL means the statistical evidence does not meet the configured requirements.
